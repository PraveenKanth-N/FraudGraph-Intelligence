## Brand & Style

The visual narrative bridges two disparate worlds: high-stakes institutional finance investigative journalism and high-throughput GPU kernel engineering. It creates an atmosphere of calculated stillness, forensic authority, and mathematical supremacy. The interface does not look like an off-the-shelf SaaS dashboard; it feels like an classified institutional terminal merged with a masterfully printed financial monograph.

To express this dual persona, the visual language utilizes:
- **Cinematic Dark Editorial Minimal:** Expansive obsidian and deep graphite viewports defined by sharp 1px hairline micro-borders rather than ambient drop shadows.
- **Typographic Dialectic:** Classical high-contrast serif headlines evoke the weight of editorial investigation and executive intelligence briefs, directly countered by razor-sharp, dense monospaced and sans-serif analytical readouts.
- **Luminescent Telemetry:** Extreme restraint in color application. The canvas remains strictly near-black and deep carbon, reserved solely for moments where high-velocity computational vectors, active graph nodes, and CUDA allocation metrics pierce the darkness in high-intensity electric cyan.
- **Architectural Zero-Radius Form:** Absolute architectural precision. Elements eschew consumer-friendly curves in favor of crisp, engineered right angles that convey structural permanence and technical rigor.

## Colors

The palette enforces a strict hierarchy based on cognitive signal-to-noise ratio. The baseline environment is an abyss of deep slate and cold graphite (`#05070A` to `#161B22`), creating a zero-light pollution canvas for graph inspection and matrix evaluation.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#00F2FE`):** Electric Cyan represents instantaneous compute state: activated graph nodes, real-time traversal paths, primary query triggers, and top-tier alert badges. It is never used for broad decorative backgrounds; it functions strictly as a directional energy pulse.
- **Secondary (`#0AE8B7`):** Phosphor Mint designates confirmed operational throughput, validated transaction clusters, healthy GPU tensor cores, and verified safe nodes.
- **Tertiary (`#EFECE6`):** Warm Alabaster/Cream introduces an analog, editorial contrast into documentation, whitepapers, and narrative case dossiers. When applied against dark slate, it provides exceptional reading comfort without harsh fluorescent glare.
- **Neutral (`#080B0F`):** Deep Obsidian anchors the root viewport, receding behind data tables and interactive node diagrams.

### Semantic Rules
- Active borders utilize 1px transitions from `border-hairline` (`#21262D`) to `border-active` (`#00F2FE`).
- Backgrounds must never use standard blurs without a corresponding dark neutral tint; semi-translucency always relies on `surface-graphite` at 85% opacity with 12px hardware-accelerated backdrop filter.
- Critical fraud warnings override color temperature using pure spectral red (`#FF3B30`), isolated against graphite with no ambient glow to prevent screen bleeding.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally contrasts high-culture editorial authority with technical computational velocity.

- **Editorial Headings (`Newsreader`):** Used for top-level dossier narratives, structural investigative findings, intelligence briefs, and editorial marketing claims. Its presence communicates human judgment, investigative thoroughness, and institutional gravity. Use standard or medium weights (`400`/`500`); avoid heavy weights to preserve the delicate razor-serif stroke contrast.
- **Analytical Engine (`Geist`):** Manages all structural UX, metrics, operational alerts, dense tabular data, and high-density graphs. Geist's geometric discipline and cold mechanical neutrality keep dense information legible at sizes as small as 11px.
- **Micro-Metric Telemetry (`label-code`):** All telemetry labels, GPU memory states, latency counts, and graph coordinates must be set in uppercase with tracking expanded (`0.08em`) to enforce tabular scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an engineered 12-column rigid analytical grid framed by explicit 1px coordinate axes. Negative space is deployed not to create emptiness, but to establish stark visual separation between real-time data visualizers and narrative briefs.

### Grid & Density
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column dynamic framework with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer `margin: 2.5rem`. Layout conforms to split-view orchestration: 65% canvas dedicated to WebGL/Canvas node clustering, 35% dedicated to analytical inspector panels and dossier text.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Analytical sidebars collapse into persistent bottom-docked drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem` and `margin-mobile: 1rem`. High-density metrics reflow into stacked horizontal key-value ribbons.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internal padding relies strictly on an 8-point base rhythmic scale, stepping into 4px sub-increments for compact telemetry cells (`space-xs`). Large vertical intervals (`space-xl`) are reserved for separating disparate analytical domains and demarcating narrative dossier boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

This system discards diffuse drop shadows and layered skeletal blurs typical of consumer applications. Depth is articulated purely through surface tonal luminance, optical edge reflection, and razor-fine spatial boundaries.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Abyss Layer (`surface-obsidian` - `#05070A`):** The ground plane. Houses network graph render surfaces, terminal runtimes, and canvas backgrounds.
2. **Structural Tier (`surface-graphite` - `#0D1117`):** Primary workspace panels, split-screen data grids, and document containers. Elevated via a 1px solid perimeter boundary using `#21262D`.
3. **Floating Inspector Tier (`surface-raised` - `#161B22`):** Dropdown matrices, node hover inspectors, active code modals, and alert callouts. Receives an internal top-edge highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
4. **Active Luminescence:** Rather than an omnidirectional blur, active focus and high-priority threats project a tight, directional rim glow: `0 0 0 1px #00F2FE, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.15)`.

### Translucency Protocol
Glassmorphism is restricted to floating overlay utility panels and temporal data overlays. When applied, use an 85% opacity `#0D1117` fill paired with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a crisp 1px border. Never blur over active node vectors if frame rates drop below 60 FPS.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to an uncompromising zero-radius (`0px`) structural constraint across every single element: buttons, containers, tooltips, tags, inputs, and modal canvases. 

- **Pure Orthogonal Geometry:** Every boundary terminates at an absolute 90-degree right angle. This geometry reinforces the concept of unyielding mathematical accuracy, low-level binary logic, and computational unpretentiousness.
- **Structural Insets:** Cut-corner visual motifs (chamfering at 45 degrees, 4px) are permitted only on telemetry indicators and GPU status flags to evoke precision-machined industrial hardware.
- **Micro-Lines:** Structural division relies entirely on continuous 1px strokes. Never use dashed borders unless indicating an empty state awaiting streaming tensor data.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cybernetic Button:** Strict rectangular profile. Background `#00F2FE`, foreground text `#05070A` in `Geist` Medium (13px, tracking `0.02em`). Zero border-radius. On hover, background shifts to `#0AE8B7`; active click compresses scale to `0.99` with instantaneous state switch.
- **Editorial Secondary:** Transparent background, 1px solid border `#21262D`, text `#EFECE6`. On hover, border activates to `#EFECE6` with an interior fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
- **Terminal Micro-Action:** Compact padding (`space-xs` `space-sm`), text `#484F58`, font `label-code`. On hover, foreground snaps to `#00F2FE` with zero transition lag.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Structural panels feature a background of `#0D1117`, a 1px solid `#21262D` border, and sharp corners.
- Header bars within panels are explicitly partitioned by a horizontal 1px bottom border `#21262D`, displaying the module title in `Geist` 12px uppercase alongside a monospaced status indicator.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#05070A`, border 1px `#21262D`, typography `Geist` 13px with placeholder text in `#484F58`. 
- Focused state eliminates generic glow rings; it replaces the border color with `#00F2FE` and introduces a 2px vertical cyan cursor pip at the active typing index.

### Graph Chips & Status Tags
- Zero border-radius rectangular tags.
- Default state: background `#161B22`, border 1px `#21262D`, text `#8B949E`, 11px uppercase.
- Alert/Malicious state: background `rgba(255, 59, 48, 0.1)`, border 1px `#FF3B30`, text `#FF3B30`.
- Safe/Verified state: background `rgba(10, 232, 183, 0.1)`, border 1px `#0AE8B7`, text `#0AE8B7`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Checkboxes are 14x14px absolute squares with 1px border `#21262D` and zero radius.
- When selected, the fill turns `#00F2FE` with a solid black high-contrast glyph. Radio buttons use concentric nested squares (an inner 6x6px solid square centered within the outer 14x14px boundary) instead of circles.

### Tables & Matrix Lists
- Monochromatic, compact data grids with zero outer horizontal padding.
- Rows separated by 1px hairline dividers (`#161B22`). Row hover triggers an immediate background fill of `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.02)` and shifts the row's primary metric text to `#00F2FE`.
